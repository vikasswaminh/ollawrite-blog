   - Architecture & Feature:
     Built a fixed floating pill dock at the bottom-right of the viewport enabling
     instant, 1-click live theme switching across 7 curated color palettes:
     1. Electric Rose (#F43F5E) — Modern, confident, high-contrast SaaS
     2. Deep Indigo   (#6366F1) — Academic AI research rigor & authority
     3. Cyber Azure   (#0284C7) — Deep-tech infrastructure & vector graphs
     4. Sunset Coral  (#F97316) — Warm editorial craft & publishing
     5. Amber Gold    (#D97706) — Executive, financial & legal intelligence
     6. Swiss Emerald (#10B981) — Veracity, truth & zero-hallucination audit
     7. Slate Titanium(#0F172A) — Pure monochrome minimalism
   - Technical Implementation:
     * Uses CSS variable remapping on `document.documentElement` (`data-theme-color`).
     * Instantly updates buttons, badge backgrounds, glow shadows, active borders,
       and syntax code colors in real time without page reload.
     * Persists the active color selection in browser `localStorage`.
